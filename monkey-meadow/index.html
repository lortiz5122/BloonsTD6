MONKEY MEADOW

How to Play

  1. Goal: Stop the Bloons from reaching the end of the path by placing monkey towers.
  2. Select a tower from the right sidebar (or press Q / W / E) — the card lights up gold.
  3. Place it by clicking an empty grass area on the map. Green ring = valid, red ring = blocked. Only then is cash spent. Right-click / Esc to cancel.
  4. Towers shoot automatically! You don't aim or click to fire — any Bloon that enters a tower's range gets popped. Click a placed tower to change its targeting priority.
  5. Start the round: Press Space or click Start Round. Earn $1 per pop + an end-of-round bonus.
  6. Speed up time: Press Tab or click the 1× / 2× button.
  7. Bandana styles: Select a placed Dart Monkey to cycle its bandana color — Red, Green, Blue, Purple.
  8. Survive all 20 rounds — the final round is a M.O.A.B. Good luck, monkey commander!
